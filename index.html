How to complete your details

First name — enter the name you are usually called by.

Middle name — optional. Enter it only if it is part of your full legal or preferred name.

Last name — enter your surname or family name.

Phone number — enter a number the sound-team coordinator can use if they need to contact you about a shift.

Email address — enter an address you can access. It will be used to identify your account.

Password — choose at least six characters. Never share it with another volunteer.
